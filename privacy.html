Privacy Policy

NSSS – Nursing Smart Shift System

Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how NSSS (Nursing Smart Shift System) handles information when you use the application.

Health Data

NSSS may request permission to access supported health and fitness information through Apple HealthKit and Apple Watch in order to provide supported application features, including shift-related wellness monitoring, insights, and alerts.

Health data is accessed only after the user grants the required permissions. Users can change or revoke these permissions at any time through Apple Health or device settings.

Use of Health Information

Health information accessed by NSSS is used only to provide health, wellness, and shift-related functionality requested by the user.

NSSS does not use HealthKit health data for advertising or marketing purposes.

Data Sharing

NSSS does not sell HealthKit health data to advertisers, data brokers, or other third parties.

Permissions

NSSS may request access to features such as notifications and supported Apple Health information. These permissions are controlled by the user through Apple's system settings.

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information used by the application and limit access to information required for supported application functionality.

Medical Disclaimer

NSSS is intended for informational, organizational, and wellness support purposes only. It is not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease or medical condition.

The application should not replace professional medical advice, diagnosis, treatment, or clinical judgment.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when application functionality or privacy practices change.

Contact

For privacy questions or support, please contact the NSSS support team.
