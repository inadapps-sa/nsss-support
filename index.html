Nursing Smart Shift System

About NSSS

NSSS (Nursing Smart Shift System) is designed to assist users with monitoring and managing nursing shifts.

The system can use supported Apple Watch and HealthKit data to provide shift-related information, wellness insights, alerts, and recommendations.

Technical Support

If you experience an issue while using NSSS, support is available for:

  • Apple Watch connectivity
  • HealthKit permissions
  • Heart rate and supported health data
  • Notifications and alerts
  • Shift monitoring
  • Application functionality
  • Technical problems and bug reports

Apple Watch & HealthKit

NSSS may access supported health information through Apple Health and Apple Watch after receiving permission from the user.

Health permissions can be reviewed or changed at any time through the Health app or device settings.

Notifications

NSSS may provide notifications and alerts based on supported application features and available data.

Notification availability may depend on device permissions, Apple Watch connectivity, and system settings.

Contact Support

If you need assistance or would like to report a technical issue, contact the NSSS support team.

Medical Disclaimer

NSSS is intended for informational, organizational, and wellness support purposes only.

NSSS is not a medical device and is not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease or medical condition.

Information, alerts, scores, and recommendations provided by NSSS should not replace professional medical advice, diagnosis, treatment, or clinical judgment.

Privacy

NSSS respects user privacy. Health information accessed through Apple Health or Apple Watch is used only to provide supported application functionality.

Users remain in control of HealthKit permissions through Apple's privacy and health settings.
